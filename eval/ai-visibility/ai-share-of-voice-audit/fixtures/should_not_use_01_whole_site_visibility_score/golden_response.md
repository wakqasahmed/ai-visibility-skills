This request is out of scope for an AI share of voice audit.

Share of voice measures how often a brand is mentioned and cited in operator-supplied answer-engine transcripts, relative to a tracked competitor cohort. It does not score a site's own crawler access, schema coverage, sitemap health, or content depth, and it produces no whole-site score.

Delegate this to `ai-visibility-audit`, which owns whole-site visibility scoring and pillar orchestration. Once that audit identifies a brand-presence gap, come back here with pasted transcripts to quantify the share of voice behind it.
