This is a different metric that happens to share the name, so it does not apply to this skill.

What you are describing is traditional organic search share of voice: ranking positions weighted by search volume, computed from a rank-tracker export across tracked keywords. This skill measures answer-engine share of voice — how often a brand is mentioned and cited inside operator-supplied ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and AI Overview transcripts, with a cohort-scoped mention denominator. The two denominators are not comparable and cannot be blended into one figure.

Use your rank-tracking or traditional-SEO tooling for the keyword-visibility calculation. If you want the answer-engine measurement instead, paste answer transcripts and this skill can compute mention and citation share over that corpus.
